# Privacy Policy

This policy covers **milton-hermes**, a personal Google OAuth app used by one
person (Victor Tolbert) to let a self-hosted AI agent on his own computer read
and manage his own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets,
Contacts).

- **Who uses it:** only the owner. It is not offered to the public.
- **What it accesses:** only the Google data of the account that signs in,
  limited to the scopes approved on the consent screen.
- **Where data goes:** the data is processed on the owner's own machine and is
  sent to the AI model provider he chose in order to complete the requests he
  makes. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else.
- **Storage:** OAuth tokens are stored locally on that machine. Revoke access at
  any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Questions: open an issue on this repository.
